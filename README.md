**GeoNav** is a FOSS, privacy-first Android navigation platform designed to provide powerful maps, navigation, offline mapping, smart search, and deep Android integration.

## 🚧 Early Development

GeoNav is currently in the planning and early development stage.

The goal is to build a modern mapping experience that combines powerful navigation features with **privacy, openness, offline support, and user control**.

## ✨ Planned

* 🗺️ Detailed worldwide maps
* 🧭 Turn-by-turn navigation
* 📡 Advanced positioning and location fusion
* 📴 Seamless offline maps and navigation
* 🔎 Powerful local and natural-language search
* 🚗 Android Auto integration
* 🚲 Driving, walking, cycling and hiking navigation
* 🚆 Public transport support
* 🛰️ Satellite and terrain mapping
* 📍 Places, businesses and points of interest
* 📦 GPX, KML, GeoJSON and other data formats
* 🔗 Map-link importing and exporting
* 🔒 Privacy-first, local-first design
* 🛠️ Open and customizable architecture

## 🌍 Built With

GeoNav is planned around open technologies and data sources such as **OpenStreetMap** and **MapLibre**, while keeping the architecture flexible for additional providers and self-hosted infrastructure.

## 🔐 Philosophy

GeoNav aims to give users control over their maps and data.

No mandatory account.
No ads.
No unnecessary tracking.
No ecosystem lock-in.
